# SmartVision — Presentation Script

> **How to use this document**
> Each numbered section is one slide. For every slide you get:
> - **Slide title** — put this on screen.
> - **On screen** — the short bullets / visuals to show.
> - **Say this** — the narration to read aloud (professional, but easy and friendly).
>
> Total length: ~8–10 minutes at a relaxed pace. Feel free to cut slides 11–13 for a 5-minute "lightning" version.

## Slide 5 — Automatic Gate Recognition (the star feature)

**On screen**
- Simple flow diagram: **Camera reads plate → SmartVision matches it → Access recorded automatically**
- Two small labels: "Works with camera folders" and "Works with live camera feeds"

**Say this**
> Here's the feature people get most excited about. SmartVision can read license plates automatically. You connect it to your existing gate cameras — either the camera drops a photo into a folder, or it streams data over the network — and the moment a plate is detected, SmartVision recognizes it, matches it to a registered vehicle, and records the entry or exit on its own. No one has to type anything. Your gate becomes smart without replacing your hardware. And if you ever need to, you can still log an event by hand in two clicks — the manual controls are always right there.

**On screen**

**Say this**

**On screen**

**Say this**
> Room keys are a classic headache, so we made them foolproof. ### Appendix — Quick FAQ (keep in your back pocket)

- **"Do we need new cameras?"** — No. SmartVision works with your existing cameras, whether they save photos to a folder or stream over the network.
- **"How hard is it to install?"** — It deploys as a single package and can run on your own server; setup is a matter of hours, not weeks.
- **"Is our data safe?"** — It's self-hosted, so your data stays on your infrastructure. Access is role-based, so people only see what they should.
- **"Can it handle more than one gate?"** — Yes. You can configure many gates, each with its own rules.
- **"What if the internet drops?"** — The dashboard reconnects and catches up automatically, with a background safety check so nothing is lost.
- **"Can staff still log things manually?"** — Always. Automatic recognition and quick manual actions live side by side.
