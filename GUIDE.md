# 🌟 SmartVision User Guide: The Simple Way to Manage Your Facility

Welcome to **SmartVision**! This guide will help you understand how to use our system to keep track of everyone coming in and out, manage room keys, and ensure your facility runs like clockwork. 

We’ve kept it simple—no tech-speak, just clear steps on how to do your job effectively.

---

## 🚦 1. The Dashboard: Your Command Center

Think of the Dashboard as your "Quick Glance" screen. It tells you exactly what’s happening **right now**.

### 📊 The Big Numbers (Top of the screen)
- **Vehicles Inside**: How many cars or trucks are currently parked or moving inside your facility.
- **People Inside**: How many individuals are currently checked in.
- **Keys Distributed**: How many room keys are currently "out" with employees.

### 🚪 Live Gate Feed Cards
These boxes show you the **last event** recorded by your gates.
- **Ingress**: The last vehicle that entered.
- **Checkpoint**: The last vehicle that passed an internal checkpoint.
- **Egress**: The last vehicle that left.

### ⚡ Quick Actions (The Magic Buttons)
Use these buttons to record events manually if the gates miss something:
- **Add Vehicle Access**: Record a car coming in.
- **Add User Access**: Record a person walking in or out.
- **Distribute Key**: Give a room key to an employee.
- **Collect Key**: Get a room key back from an employee.

---

## 🔑 2. Managing Room Keys

We make sure you never lose a key again.

### How to Give a Key (Distribute)
1. Click **Distribute Key**.
2. **Search Employee**: Start typing the name of the employee. The system only shows "Employees," so you won't accidentally pick a guest.
3. **Select Room**: Start typing the room number or name.
4. **Notes**: Add why they need it (optional).
5. Click **Distribute**. The "Keys Distributed" counter will go up!

### How to Get a Key Back (Collect)
1. Click **Collect Key**.
2. Pick the employee and the room.
3. Click **Collect**. The key is now marked as "Available" again.

---

## 🏢 3. Rooms & Groups

In the **Rooms** section, you can see all your rooms at once.
- **Groups**: Rooms are organized into groups (like "North Wing" or "Office Area").
- **Key Status**: A green tag means the key is **Available**. A yellow tag means it's **Collected** (someone has it).
- **Manage**: You can add new rooms or edit existing ones here.

---

## 🚗 4. Vehicles & People

### Vehicles
The **Vehicles** tab lists every vehicle registered in the system.
- You can search by license plate.
- Every vehicle can have an **Owner** (a person or company) attached to it.

### Users (People)
There are three types of people in SmartVision:
1. **Employees**: Staff members who can take room keys.
2. **Persons**: General visitors or guests.
3. **Companies**: Businesses (like delivery services) that visit frequently.

---

## 📜 5. Access Logs & Reports

Want to know who was here last Tuesday?
1. Go to **Access Logs**.
2. Use the **Search** box to find a specific license plate or name.
3. View the **Direction** (In, Out, or Checkpoint) and the **Time** they were there.
4. **Reports**: You can generate and download reports if you need a printed copy of the logs for your files.

---

## 👤 6. Roles: What Can I Do?

The system has two main types of users:

### 🛠️ The Administrator (Admin)
- Can do **everything**.
- Can add/edit/delete rooms, vehicles, and people.
- Can see all reports.
- Can manage other system settings.

### 🎮 The Operator
- Focused on daily tasks.
- Can record vehicle/user access.
- Can distribute and collect keys.
- Can view logs but usually cannot delete important data.

---

## 💡 Pro Tips for Success
- **Use the Search**: In almost every list, you can just start typing to find what you need. It’s the fastest way to work!
- **Watch the Colors**: 
  - **Green** usually means "Welcome" or "Available".
  - **Red** usually means "Left" or "Closed".
  - **Yellow/Orange** means "Pending" or "In Use".
- **Real-time Updates**: The dashboard updates itself! You don't need to refresh the page to see new events as they happen.
